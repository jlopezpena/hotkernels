On Apple Silicon, `w -= b` and `w += b` on `float64` arrays of 131,072 elements or more are much faster right after a large matrix product has read `w`, as in the gradient step `w -= lr * g` of a training loop: in such a loop on a 784 × 256 matrix the step went from about 460 µs to about 95 µs, and the program from 260 ms to 153 ms. Elsewhere they run at about NumPy's speed, and the result is NumPy's bit for bit.
