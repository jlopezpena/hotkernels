`np.bincount` on int16 and int64 arrays is served by assembly on Apple Silicon as well: on int16 codes from 0 to 63 about 9x faster than NumPy at a million elements on an Apple M1 Pro, and on int64 about 4x, where the kernels alone are 2.95x and 1.54x faster than the compiled C loops that served the calls before.
