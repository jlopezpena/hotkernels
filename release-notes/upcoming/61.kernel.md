`np.compress(mask, x)` and `np.extract(mask, x)` on `float32` and `float64`, `np.putmask(x, mask, v)` and `np.copyto(x, v, where=mask)` on `float64` with a Python number as the value, and `np.where(mask)` with only a boolean mask are served by the same kernels as `x[mask]`, `x[mask] = v` and `np.nonzero`. At a million elements with half the mask selected, `np.compress` is about 6x faster than NumPy, `np.putmask` and `np.copyto` about 18x to 20x, and `np.where(mask)` about 3.4x.
