`np.argsort(x, kind="stable")` on one-dimensional `float64`, `int64` and `uint64` arrays is served by a kernel, as are `kind="mergesort"` and `stable=True`: about 13x faster than NumPy at a million `float64` elements and 15x to 28x on integers, measured on an Apple M1 Pro. The result is NumPy's, ties, NaNs and zeros of both signs included. The default kind, `np.argsort(x)`, is unchanged.
