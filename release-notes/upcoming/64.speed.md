`np.clip` and `x.clip` on `float32` and `float64` are much faster on small arrays when the bounds are Python numbers (or NumPy scalars of the array's own type) and there is no `out`: about 10x faster than NumPy at 16 elements, where they used to gain nothing. `x.argmax()` and `x.argmin()` on arrays shorter than 128 elements are served by the kernels too: about 3x faster at 16 elements.
