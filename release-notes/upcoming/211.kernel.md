When bottleneck is installed, its `nanmean`, `nanstd`, `nanmin` and `nanmax` on two-dimensional `float64` arrays along an axis whose other axis is contiguous (`axis=0` of a C-ordered array or of a rolling-window view), which pandas and empyrical call in place of NumPy's, are served by kernels with bottleneck's results bit for bit: about 4x to 7x faster than bottleneck at 252 × 4,789 on an Apple M1 Pro. hotkernels never imports bottleneck, and `install(bottleneck=False)` leaves it alone.
