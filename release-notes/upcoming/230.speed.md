`np.median` on `float64` arrays of more than 128 elements, and `np.median(x, axis=None)` at every size, are now served by hotkernels: about 8x faster than NumPy at a million elements of random data on an Apple M1 Pro. On data that is already in order the gain is smaller, and on sorted data with a little noise added it is slower, 0.74x at a million elements.
