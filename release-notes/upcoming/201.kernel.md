`np.argmax(m, axis=1)` and `np.argmin(m, axis=1)` (and `m.argmax(axis=1)`) on a C-ordered two-dimensional `float32` or `float64` array are served: about 3.5x faster than NumPy on 300,000 rows of ten `float64` and about 15x on rows of two or three, on an Apple M1 Pro. `report()` now counts such a call once, however many rows NumPy splits it into.
