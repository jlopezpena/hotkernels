On Apple Silicon, `np.fmax.accumulate` on `float64` and `np.fmax.reduceat` read the array more cheaply before the kernel: on an Apple M1 Pro `np.fmax.accumulate` is about 18x faster than NumPy at 4,096 elements (was 7.5x) and `np.fmax.reduceat` on runs of 16 about 3x at a million (was 2.3x). `np.bincount` on 16 `int16` elements also gets faster, 4.5x where it was 3.2x.
