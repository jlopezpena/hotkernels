`m.any(axis=1)` and `np.any(m, axis=1)` on a two-dimensional boolean array in C order are served by a kernel: on rows of 22 columns about 42x faster than NumPy at a million elements and 5x on a single row, measured on x86-64. `m.any()`, other axes and calls with `out`, `keepdims` or `where` stay with NumPy.
