`np.where(mask, a, b)` on `float64` and `int64` arrays of one shape, and on a `float64` array beside a single number such as `np.nan` or `0`, is served by a kernel: measured on x86-64, about 6x faster than NumPy at a million elements with half the mask True, and 2.5x for `np.where(mask, a, np.nan)` with most of it True. On ARM64 the same calls run a plain C loop for now.
