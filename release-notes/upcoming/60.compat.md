hotkernels now requires NumPy 2.3 or later. NumPy 2.0 to 2.2 answer `np.clip` (the sign of a zero) and `np.count_nonzero` (a Python `int` instead of `np.intp`) differently from later releases, and with hotkernels installed those calls gave the later answer; on those releases `install()` now does nothing and `report()` says why.
